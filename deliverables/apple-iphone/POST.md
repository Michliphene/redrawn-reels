## PINNED COMMENT (post it right after publishing, then pin)
Sources: Apple Form 10-K, fiscal 2026 (year ended 26 Sep 2026, SEC). "Left after costs" = gross margin: 39.3% on devices ($139.7B), 76.2% on services ($93.9B), $233.6B in total. Apple doesn't disclose the iPhone on its own, so the $1,000 is the average of all Apple devices (our calculation). Services = 26% of sales, 40% of gross profit (our calculation). Before R&D and other expenses. Device margin was helped by tariff refunds this year (36.8% in fiscal 2025). Devices still make more gross profit in dollars than services.
