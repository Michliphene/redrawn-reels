## PINNED COMMENT (post it right after publishing, then pin)
Sources: ambergris forms in the sperm whale's gut, can drift for years, was used as a fixative and is now mostly replaced by synthetic Ambroxan (Wikipedia "Ambergris", MFA CAMEO) · deer musk comes from a gland of the male musk deer, protected since 1979 (CITES); almost all perfume musk is synthetic (Wikipedia "Musk") · castoreum comes from beavers' castor sacs, used for leather notes; synthetic bases replace it (Wikipedia "Castoreum") · ~1 t of jasmine flowers for 1 kg of oil, yields vary (NPR, 2021) · most rose oil comes from Bulgaria and Turkey (market data, shares vary by year) · vanilla is hand-pollinated, a method found in 1841 by Edmond Albius, 12, enslaved on Réunion (Wikipedia, Nautilus, Nat Geo) · ~half the world's vetiver oil comes from Haiti (AP, Givaudan).
