## DESCRIPTION (TikTok / Instagram)
A perfume you can't buy: kyphi, burned at dusk in Egyptian temples. 🏛️

Its recipe is carved on the walls of the temple of Edfu. Plutarch lists 16 ingredients: honey, wine, raisins, myrrh, resin, juniper… even bitumen.
The catch? Ancient writers never agree on the list (10, 15, even 36), and some of the plants can't be identified. Every kyphi made today is a reconstruction.
