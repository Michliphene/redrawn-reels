## YOUTUBE SHORTS DESCRIPTION
Bulgarian rose oil sells for about $12,000 a kilo. It takes 3 to 5 tonnes of hand-picked Damask rose petals (most sources: about 3.5) to make one kilo, picked before sunrise in the Rose Valley around Kazanlak. In 2025 farmers were paid about €1.28 per kilo of flowers; 3.5 tonnes ≈ €4,500 (our calculation).
