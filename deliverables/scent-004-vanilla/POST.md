## YOUTUBE SHORTS DESCRIPTION
Vanilla is an orchid from Mexico, and outside Mexico it almost never sets pods on its own. Each flower opens for a single day. In 1841, on Réunion, a 12-year-old enslaved boy, Edmond, found a fast way to pollinate it by hand with a thin stick and his thumb. Nearly all vanilla is still pollinated this way. He was free in 1848, took the name Albius, and died poor in 1880. Vanilla is now the second most expensive spice, after saffron.

Sources: Wikipedia, Linnean Society, Europeana, BlackPast, UF/IFAS, PLoS ONE (2024). Charles Morren hand-pollinated vanilla earlier (1836–37) but his method was not adopted. Images: AI illustrations (Google Nano Banana, OpenAI gpt-image). 3D set made in raea.
