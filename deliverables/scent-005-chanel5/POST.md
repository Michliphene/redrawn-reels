## YOUTUBE SHORTS DESCRIPTION
In 1920 perfumer Ernest Beaux showed Coco Chanel vials numbered 1 to 5 and 20 to 24; she picked the fifth, and Chanel N°5 launched in 1921. Why the fifth is a legend: her lucky number, or the clean, soapy smell of its aldehydes. Its jasmine comes from Grasse (the Mul family, since 1987). In 1952 Marilyn Monroe told LIFE she slept in "a few drops" of it, and a 1960 recording of her surfaced in a 2013 Chanel ad. MoMA added the bottle to its collection in 1959.
