Oud comes from Aquilaria trees in South and Southeast Asia. Healthy wood barely smells: a wound and a fungus make the tree flood its wood with a dark resin. That's agarwood. In Arabic, oud just means "wood".
How many wild trees carry it? About 1 in 10 (sources vary). You can't see it from outside, so whole trees get cut down to look.

## YOUTUBE SHORTS DESCRIPTION
The best oud oil is quoted at over $30,000 a kilo. It comes from Aquilaria trees that only make their fragrant resin after a wound lets a fungus in. In the wild, only about 1 in 10 trees carries it (sources vary), so harvesters cut whole trees down to look. The main oud tree is now critically endangered (IUCN 2018) and its trade has been controlled under CITES since 1995. In one trial in Bangladesh, 15 kg of wood gave 7 ml of oil: about 2 kg of wood per millilitre (our calculation).

Sources: Frontiers in Plant Science, TRAFFIC, IUCN, CITES, J. Bangladesh Agric. Univ. (Islam et al. 2014), Forests (2024), price quotes from Première Peau and Crown Agarwood Group. Images: AI-generated (Google Nano Banana), not photos. 3D set made in raea.
