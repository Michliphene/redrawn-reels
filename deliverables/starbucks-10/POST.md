## DESCRIPTION (TikTok / Instagram)
Of every $10 you pay Starbucks, they keep 97¢. ☕

The rest: $3.23 for the cup, milk, beans and food, $2.69 for the baristas (almost three times what Starbucks keeps), 80¢ of rent, $1.05 for ads and delivery apps, $1.26 for the head office, equipment and closing stores.
What's in your cup is about a third. Running the coffeehouse costs almost half.
