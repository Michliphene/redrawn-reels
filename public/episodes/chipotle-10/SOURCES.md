## Script (voice)
1. You pay Chipotle $10. The food? Under $3.
2. So where does the rest go? Let's cut it open.
3. Food, drinks and packaging: $2.96.
4. Almost as much pays the crew who make it: $2.51.
5. Rent on the restaurant? Just 52 cents.
6. $1.47 runs the store: delivery, ads, card fees, power.
7. 92 cents for the head office, equipment and new stores.
8. And Chipotle keeps $1.62.
9. Across 4,000 restaurants, that's $1.9 billion of operating profit in 2025.
10. So the food is under a third. The restaurant around it costs nearly twice as much.
11. Remember that next time… → (loop) You pay Chipotle $10.

## Simplifications and debated points (be ready for comments)
- **"Keeps" = operating profit**, before interest income and tax. After tax, net income is 12.9 % ($1.29 per $10).
  Say so in the pinned comment if asked.
- **Averages across the company**, not one burrito. The split of one bowl is not disclosed.
- **"$10" is a unit, not a price.** No claim about what a burrito costs.
- **Delivery orders**: delivery revenue is 0.5 % of revenue; delivery fees paid to apps sit in "other operating costs".
- **No claim that prices are too high or that margins are unusual.** No comparison with other chains is sourced here.
- Not financial advice.
