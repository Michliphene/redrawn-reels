# EP 7 — The split nobody was asked about (Czechoslovakia, 1989–1993) · sources & fact sheet

Format: **A · explainer with a stake hook** ("nobody asked them") and a payoff twist (the flag both sides promised
not to keep — the Czechs kept it). Topic #20 of the bible ("How Czechoslovakia split without a shot"), angle
sharpened at scripting.

## Script (as spoken)
1. 15 million people. One country. Split in two, and nobody asked them.
2. 1989: the Velvet Revolution ends communist rule in Czechoslovakia.
3. In 1990, the fights begin. First, over a hyphen in the country's name.
4. '92: Václav Klaus wins in Prague, Vladimír Mečiar in Bratislava. Their plans don't fit.
5. They agree to split. No referendum.
6. In polls, only about one in three, on either side, wants it.
7. January 1, 1993: Czechoslovakia ends. Peacefully.
8. Everything is shared two to one: tanks, planes, trains.
9. The gold takes until 1999.
10. One rule: neither side keeps the old flag.
11. The Czechs keep it anyway.
12. Two countries. One old flag. All decided for… → (loop) "15 million people."

Wording choices: "Their plans don't fit. They agree to split." states two facts side by side; no single cause is
claimed (historians weigh economics, nationalism and the two leaders differently). "Nobody asked them" = no
referendum was held (sourced); the 1992 elections are not presented as a vote on the split.

## Debated / limits (logged)
- Title "nobody was asked about" = no referendum. The split itself was voted by the Federal Assembly (Constitutional Act 542/1992, 25 Nov 1992) — so the title never says "nobody voted".
- Names: "Czech Republic" (the 1993 name) rather than "Czechia" (English short name adopted 2016).
- Causes of the split: debated → no cause stated.
- Poll figures: 36/37 % swapped between sources → shown as "1 in 3".
- Hyphen War dates differ slightly between sources → only "1990" in voice; header 29 Mar 1990.
- Header date for the poll beat: "Sept 1992" (day not given by the source; the engine shows 1 SEP).
- Tanks on the map are symbolic (2 vs 1), not an inventory.
- Borders: today's Czech–Slovak border from Natural Earth (the 1993 line; later minor adjustments are below 1:50m).
