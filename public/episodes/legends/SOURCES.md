# UNGRADED #004 — Never cut

Format: D. myth-bust, three short stories everyone knows, each with a twist. Notebook art direction
(`composition: Doodle`). Audience: everyone (teens to thirties). Tone: simple, motivating, reassuring.
Message: their famous failures were smaller than the legends, so yours is normal and not the end.

## Script (voice) — about 70 s (owner's rule: every reel > 60 s)

## Debated points
- The Jordan "cut" story is his own, and he meant how it felt; we don't call him a liar, we say "that's how it felt".
- Harry Potter's rejection count: we say "about twelve, by most counts".
- Sanders: 62 vs 65; the first deal (1952) is the 62.
