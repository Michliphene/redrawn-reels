## Script (13 beats)
1. Perfume isn't just flowers. Some of it came from a whale's gut.
2. It's called ambergris. A sperm whale makes it, and it can float at sea for years before it washes up.
3. Perfumers loved it because it makes a scent last on your skin.
4. Musk? It first came from a gland of the male musk deer. Since 1979, the deer is protected.
5. And the leather note in old classics? Castoreum. From beavers. Yes, beavers.
6. Here's the twist: today, almost all of these animal notes are made in a lab.
7. The flowers, though, are still real. And it takes a lot of them.
8. One kilo of jasmine oil needs about a ton of flowers, picked by hand around Grasse, France.
9. Most of the world's rose oil comes from two countries: Bulgaria and Turkey.
10. Vanilla is pollinated by hand, one flower at a time. An enslaved twelve-year-old, Edmond Albius, found the trick in 1841.
11. And about half the world's vetiver oil comes from Haiti. From the roots.
12. Whales, deer and beavers, rebuilt in a lab. A ton of flowers, picked by hand. That's what's in the bottle.
13. So next time you spray it, remember: → loops into line 1.
