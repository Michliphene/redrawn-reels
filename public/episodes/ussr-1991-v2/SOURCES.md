# Ep.1 — How 1 country became 15 · sources & fact sheet

## Script (as spoken)
1. The largest country on Earth fell apart in under two years.
2. March 1990: Lithuania goes first, declaring its independence restored.
3. Moscow refuses. In January 1991, Soviet troops attack the TV tower in Vilnius. Fourteen civilians die.
4. In April, Georgia follows.
5. Then, in August, hardliners try to overthrow Gorbachev. The coup fails in three days.
6. Estonia and Latvia break away in the chaos.
7. Within ten days: Ukraine, Belarus, Moldova, Azerbaijan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan.
8. By October: Tajikistan, Armenia, Turkmenistan.
9. December 8: Russia, Ukraine and Belarus sign the Belavezha Accords.
10. The Soviet Union, they declare, no longer exists.
11. Kazakhstan is the last to declare, on December 16.
12. December 25: Gorbachev resigns. The Soviet flag comes down over the Kremlin.
13. Fifteen countries from one. And even on its own, Russia is still… → (loop) "the largest country on Earth".

## Debated / nuanced points (kept neutral)
- **When did the USSR end?** 8 Dec (Belavezha), 21 Dec (Alma-Ata Protocol), 25 Dec (Gorbachev resigns) or 26 Dec (Soviet of the Republics votes dissolution). Script states events, not "the end date".
- **Baltic states:** they consider themselves *restored* states (1940 annexation not recognised by the US and many Western states); hence "declaring its independence restored".
- **Vilnius, 13 Jan 1991:** 14 civilians killed per Lithuanian authorities and mainstream reporting; some reports say 13 killed in the assault (+1 related death). Russian state-linked claims deny responsibility — classed as disinformation by EUvsDisinfo. Lithuanian courts convicted Soviet officers (2019).
- **Causes of the collapse** (economy, nationalism, Gorbachev's reforms, the coup): debated — **the script deliberately does not attribute a cause.**
